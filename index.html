THE MEASURES BEHIND THE VIEW

Definitions you can audit.

All views use the supplied 2021 data. Calls are aggregate records; surveys are individual responses.

Answered Calls
Within service level + answers after service level.
Total handle time
Talk + Hold + After-Call Work. Source units are not specified; seconds are assumed.
AHT, minutes
SUM(handle time) / SUM(answered calls) / 60.
PCA
SUM(answered) / SUM(offered).
SLA
SUM(within service level) / SUM(offered). No threshold or target was supplied.
Average calls/day
Offered calls / selected Calendar dates, including zero-volume dates for a selected segment.
CSAT
Mean rating: Very Dissatisfied=1, Dissatisfied=2, Neutral=3, Satisfied=4, Very Satisfied=5.
FCR
Mean of normalized Yes=1 / No=0 responses. Unrecognized responses are excluded rather than treated as No.
Monthly association
Pearson correlation of equally weighted monthly FCR/CSAT means. Samples and association do not establish causation.

Language comparisons retain both teams and follow Month + Skill. Skill comparisons retain both skills and follow Month + Language. Surveys have no Language, Skill, or Call ID and use date only. Boxplot observations are daily weighted averages, not individual-call durations.
